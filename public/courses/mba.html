Eligibility

Online MBA Eligibility Criteria

To pursue an online MBA degree, it is necessary to look into the eligibility criteria before considering any institution. Please note that the eligibility is different for different universities and specialisations; therefore, below is a general criteria required to enrol in an online MBA course:

  • Educational Criteria: Students must have a bachelor's degree or other equivalent degree in any field from a recognised university. Students must hold a minimum of 50% (45% for reserved categories) aggregate score.
  • Entrance exams: Even though most universities do not require students to appear for an entrance exam in the case of an online MBA, some may require it. Some of the popular entrance exams considered by institutions for online MBAs are XAT, CAT, MAT, CMAT, SNAP, and other management entrance examinations. Apart from this, personal interviews may also be conducted in certain institutes.
  • Entrance exams: Additional Considerations: There is no age limit for enrolling in an online MBA course. If a candidate has relevant work experience, it will be a great advantage. Students studying in the final year of graduation are also eligible to apply for the online MBA Programme. Some colleges expect you to have 2-3 years of work experience for an online MBA course.
Career Opportunities

Career Opportunities After Online MBA

The successful completion of the Online MBA course opens gateway to multiple opportunities in various sectors. We have mentioned some of the best career prospects you can explore after pursuing the Online MBA:

Job Roles Job Description
Business Analyst A Business Analyst understands and analyses the problems and opportunities in market and makes data-driven solutions to maintain business growth. They play a crucial role in improving the efficiency of the company.
Human Resource Manager This role requires skill set to hire, manage, and retain employees. They possess the capability to supervise the employee performance and act as a bridge between the management and the employee
Marketing Manager A Marketing Manager analyses the target audience, handles the marketing resources, develops strategies and implements innovative ideas to strenghthen the marketing plans of the organisation.
Project Manager With excellent communication skills, a Project Manager assigns the tasks of the project to the employees of the company and takes care of the entire project flow.
Entrepreneur With a good knowledge of strategy, idea development, finance, market and the current trends, etc., one can become an entrepreneur and launch and manage their own company.
Financial Manager Works in a variety of industries, such as banks, enterprises, firms, etc., where he/she helps in assisting the financial needs of the company by providing valuable feedback and manages and turns the entire organisation into a profitable one. He/she also gives solutions for reducing the financial risks and applies his/her investment knowledge in managing the organisation's financial resources better.
Operational Research Manager Manages the entire process of production, supplies and services of the products or goods. They are capable of finding the operational issues and manages the data gathered.
Management Consultant Identifies problems, develops strategies, consults with the organisation and provide ideas to improve the performance of the company.
