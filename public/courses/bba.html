Eligibility

Online BBA Eligibility Criteria

Every college/university providing online BBA programme has a different criteria to shortlist candidates for admission. Here is an eligibility criteria often generally followed by institutions:

  • Educational Qualification : Aspiring candidates must have successfully completed their 10+2 examinations from a recognised education board with a minimum 50% marks. For reserved category students, the minimum marks criteria can be 45% depending upon the college/university.
  • Entrance Exam : Although most of the colleges/universities do not ask for entrance test scores for online BBA programme, however, some institutions consider common entrance exams.
Career Opportunities

Career Opportunities After Online BBA

The successful completion of the Online BBA course opens gateways to multiple opportunities in various sectors. We have mentioned some of the best career prospects you can explore after pursuing the Online BBA:

Job Roles Job Description
Financial Analyst A Financial Executive finds employment in banks, pension funds, insurance companies, and other such disciplines. Their role is to assist businesses and individuals in decisions concerning the evaluation of their finances and how to increase their profit.
Marketing Executive This professional has to direct the marketing plan of the company's product or service. Their expertise is required to monitor market trends and analyze customer behavior to improve existing marketing strategies.
HR Specialist Their basic role is to screen, interview, and recruit job applicants. Apart from this, an HR Specialist handles employee training and compensation procedures.
Operations Executive This role revolves around carrying daily trade operations covering all processing stages of an order. He/she is also in charge of managing storage and distribution of products.
Supply Chain Executive A Supply Chain Executive has to analyze operations to reduce risks in the flow of goods and services from raw material stage to the final product. All this is done while keeping in mind the government and company regulations.
SEO Executive He/she is responsible to create and optimize web strategies for growing organic traffic. On a regular basis, SEO Executive tests, analyzes, and changes the website of a business so that the website ranks higher on search engines.
Sales Executive Role of a Sales Executive varies as per the industry, but mostly shares the same responsibility. He/she has to create sales quotas, analyze sales strategies, and generate leads for the company.
Financial Planner This job role involves helping clients in creating strategies and streamlined plans to achieve their long-term financial goals.
