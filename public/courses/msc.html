Eligibility

Online M.Sc Eligibility Criteria

You may find different admission process and eligibility criteria in different colleges. Therefore, we have provided a general criteria followed by most of the institutions:

  • A bachelor's degree in a relevant field is usually required. For example, if you're applying for an MSc in Computer Science, a bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field is generally preferred but not compulsory.
  • Many universities specify a GPA or minimum marks of 50% to apply for the programme.
Career Opportunities

Career Opportunities After Online M.Sc

Earning a Master of Science (M.Sc) degree opens up a variety of career opportunities across different industries. The specific career paths depend on the field of study and specialization within the M.Sc program.

Job Roles Job Description
Chemical Analyst A professional who is proficient to assess the chemical nature and structure of various substances.
Subject Teacher/Lecturer Teachers are employed in schools or private tuition centres to teach the particular subject you have specialised in.
Research Assistant They work in labs to perform experiments and investigate science problems related to topics such as bioscience, chemistry, geoscience, and many others.
IT Consultant Responsible to design, instruct, and operate the technical network and system of an organization. They may work on troubleshooting issues concerning software, hardware, and information systems of the organisation.
Quality Control Analyst He/she has to examine the product manufactured by the company to meet the quality and safety standards of the client or consumers.
Clinical Psychologist An expert individual who is specialised in providing comprehensive mental and behavioural health to individuals, couples, etc. They can work with an agency or individually.
Financial Analyst A candidate skilled to analyse the company's financial performance and determine the opportunities for profit improvement.
