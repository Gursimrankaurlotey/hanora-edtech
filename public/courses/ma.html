Eligibility

Online MA Eligibility Criteria

A generic eligibility criteria for online MA is as follows:

  • Bachelor's Degree : Most online MA programmes require a bachelor's degree in a relevant field from a recognised university. Minimum required marks vary between universities and specialisations, typically ranging from 45% to 55%.
  • Specific Subject Requirements : Some programmes may require specific subjects in your bachelor's degree relevant to the chosen master's program. Please note that not many colleges and universities have this requirement.
Career Opportunities

Career Opportunities After Online MA

Career prospects you will have after pursuing the online MA programme rely on the area of specialization you choose and interest. The job roles that open up for a MA graduate include the following:

Job Roles Job Description
Professor As a subject matter expert, teaches undergraduate and graduate courses in the field of expertise. Additionally, conducts research and publishes scholarly articles and papers.
Museum Curator Oversees the collection, preservation, and exhibition of art and artifacts at a museum or gallery. Researches and interprets historical and cultural objects for public education and enjoyment.
Writer/Editor Writes for publications such as newspapers, magazines, or online platforms. As an editor, edits and proofreads the work of others. Roles may vary depending on the organization.
Artist Engaged in creating various forms of art pieces and exhibits them to the public. The form of art created defines the niche of an artist.
Social Worker A trained professional who works with populations suffering from poverty, discrimination, and other social injustices. Provides counseling and support while advocating for social justice and policy change.
School Counselor Provides academic, social, and emotional support to students in elementary, middle, or high school.
Psychologist Conducts research, diagnoses and treats mental illness, and provides therapy to individuals and groups.
Teacher Appointed for middle or high school students to teach subjects mastered. Develops lesson plans and assessments, and creates a stimulating learning environment.
Journalist Researches and writes news articles for newspapers, magazines, TV news channels, and digital platforms. In this process, interviews various sources to gather correct factual information.
Public Relations Specialist Responsible for managing and improving the public image of an organization or individual by developing and implementing strategic PR campaigns.

Whether an online MA is worth it depends on your individual circumstances and career goals. If you are a working professional or engulfed in familial responsibilities, pursuing an online MA degree is totally worth it.
