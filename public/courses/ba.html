Career Opportunities

Career Opportunities After Online BA

Depending upon individual skillset and interests, candidates can explore various job roles in this field.

Job Roles Job Description
Librarian Manage and maintain all aspects of a library, including collection, organisation, circulation, reference services, user education, and program planning. They have to address the user inquiries and material database of the library.
Indexer A professional who reviews various documents and makes information search easier for the readers by using specific methodologies.
Teaching Assistant Works in collaboration with a lead teacher and helps reinforce lesson comprehension. Along with this, they have to assist the lead teacher in other aspects as well.
Library Technician This role will vary as per the size of the library or store. They are the point of contact for any sort of information, collection of library materials, re-shelving, and issuing library cards.
Archivist Preserve and manage historical records, documents, and artifacts in archives and museums.
