University Overview

About Sharda University Online

Established in 2009, Sharda University is one of India's leading private universities and is ranked among the top private institutions for management education. Over the years, the university has earned recognition for its academic excellence, modern infrastructure, and industry-focused curriculum.

To make quality higher education accessible to a wider audience, Sharda University established its Centre for Distance and Open Learning. Through its online learning platform, the university enables students and working professionals to pursue industry-relevant degree programs without compromising their personal or professional commitments.

Sharda University Online offers a diverse range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs at affordable fee structures. The online degrees are widely recognized and accepted, helping learners acquire valuable knowledge, enhance professional skills, and advance their careers through flexible and accessible education.

Admission Process

The admission process for Sharda University Online is conducted completely online. Admissions generally begin in January every year and students can apply directly without appearing for any entrance examination. The admission process is simple and can be completed in a few easy steps.

1

Visit the official website of Sharda University Online.

2

Select your preferred online course.

3

Pay the programme fee through the online payment portal.

4

Wait for admission confirmation from the university.
