University Overview

About Online Uttaranchal University (Online UU)

Established in 2013, Uttaranchal University is a renowned private university located in Uttarakhand. Over the years, the university has made significant contributions to higher education and has earned recognition from various accreditation bodies. Its commitment to academic excellence has been acknowledged with the prestigious NAAC A+ Grade accreditation.

To make quality education accessible to a larger audience, the university launched Online Uttaranchal University (Online UU). The university offers a variety of UGC-approved online degree programmes including MBA, MCA, BBA, BCA, and BA. Online UU degrees are also recognized by AICTE, AIU, and WES, making them widely accepted both in India and internationally.

The fee structure of Online UU courses ranges between ₹72,000 and ₹1,40,000. The university provides scholarships, EMI facilities, advanced e-learning resources, and a user-friendly Learning Management System to ensure a seamless learning experience. Students also receive dedicated placement support through the Career Resource Centre (CRC), which assists with resume building, interview preparation, and connections with top recruiters through extensive corporate partnerships.

ADMISSION PROCESS

Admission Process

Online Uttaranchal University conducts admissions primarily through online mode. Admissions generally take place in January and July sessions. Students, professionals, homemakers, and entrepreneurs can apply directly by following the admission process outlined below.

1

Visit the official Online UU website and register your account.

2

Apply for your preferred online programme.

3

4

5

6

7
