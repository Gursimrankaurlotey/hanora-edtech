University Overview

About Jain University Online

Established in 1990, Jain University was conferred the “Deemed-to-be-University” status by the UGC in 2009. Today, the university has prospered into 7 thriving campuses with thousands of learners currently admitted to different programmes. Jain University introduced its e-learning platform — Jain Online to serve as a one-stop destination for the educational needs of all students. The university is constantly working towards establishing a conducive online learning atmosphere. At Jain University Online, the courses encompass a range of in-demand disciplines. It includes management, arts and humanities, commerce, and computer science & applications.

Jain online courses are approved by the UGC and AICTE. Additionally, the university also has WES recognition, enhancing the global acceptance of its online degrees and certificates. Popular courses at Jain Online include an online MBA, BBA, MCA, and BCA. Furthermore, Jain University Online courses fees range between INR 15,000 and INR 2,98,000. Apart from this, the university offers various career assistance services powered by the “foundit” portal. Placement support facilities include building professional resumes, interview preparation guides, job alerts on featured profiles, etc. With 78% of career success rate, the NAAC A++ university helps you advance in your career with 1000+ hiring corporations.

ADMISSION PROCESS

Admission Process

Jain University Online admissions are conducted through online mode. The admission windows may open several times a year, with fresh admissions beginning in January. Besides, to apply for Jain online courses, you do not need to take any entrance exam, owing to direct admission approaches. Explore the step-by-step admission process for Jain University online courses:

1

Visit the Jain Online official website & create your account.

2

Navigate to your desired course & apply for it.

3

Fill out the application form with essential information.

4

Upload the scanned images of vital documents.

5

Pay the registration fee & submit the form.

6

Upon confirmation by Jain University Online, pay the course fee online & Done.
