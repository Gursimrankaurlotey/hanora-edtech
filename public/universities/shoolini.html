University Overview

About Shoolini University Online

Shoolini University was established in 2009 by leading academicians and professionals with a vision to create a research-driven institution. It is a young and philanthropic university that has gained recognition for its outstanding contributions to innovation, research, and patent filings. In the NIRF Rankings 2024, Shoolini University secured the 70th position, reflecting its growing reputation in higher education.

The university is known for its internationally qualified faculty, innovative teaching methods, strong focus on student careers, and commitment to academic excellence. As a non-profit institution, Shoolini University aims to bridge the gap between traditional and online learning by providing high-quality and accessible education to learners across the globe.

Shoolini University's online and distance learning programs are UGC-entitled and delivered by experienced faculty members and industry mentors. The university offers AI-driven career support services, including career guidance sessions, resume and profile building assistance, AI-powered interview preparation, and support for developing e-portfolios and patents. Graduates from Shoolini Online have secured placements with leading organizations such as ICICI Bank, Nestlé, LG, L&T, and many other reputed companies.
