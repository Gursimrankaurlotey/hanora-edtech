University Overview

About NMIMS Centre for Distance and Online Education

The NMIMS Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE) is one of India's leading institutions for delivering career-oriented and quality higher education. All NMIMS online courses are UGC-approved and widely accepted for higher education and employment opportunities. The institution has also been awarded the prestigious NAAC A++ accreditation, reflecting its commitment to academic excellence.

Founded in 1981, NMIMS was granted the status of a Deemed-to-be University in 2003. Recognising the growing need for flexible learning among students and working professionals, the university established its distance and online learning division to provide accessible and industry-relevant education across the country. Today, NMIMS CDOE offers a wide range of online programmes in management, commerce, and professional development disciplines.

NMIMS Online provides undergraduate degrees, postgraduate degrees, diploma programmes, certification courses, and executive education through a flexible online learning model. Course fees range from ₹55,000 to ₹4,50,000. The programmes are specifically designed for students and working professionals who wish to enhance their qualifications and career prospects without interrupting their professional commitments. NMIMS continues to be recognised globally as one of India's premier institutions for management education.

ADMISSION PROCESS

Admission Process

NMIMS Online follows a bi-annual admission cycle with intakes generally conducted in January and July. Admissions are based on eligibility criteria, and no entrance examination is required. The entire admission process can be completed online.

1

Visit the official NMIMS Online website.

2

Select your preferred programme and start the application process.

3

Fill out the online application form with the required information.

4

Upload scanned copies of all required documents in the prescribed format.

5

Pay the application fee online and submit the admission form.

6

After confirmation from the university, pay the programme fee and download the fee receipt.
