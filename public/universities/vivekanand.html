University Overview

About Vivekananda Global University Online

Vivekananda Global University (VGU) is a renowned institution that has aligned its academic standards with global benchmarks. The university has been awarded the prestigious NAAC A+ accreditation for maintaining excellence in education, innovation, and student success. Along with its well-established residential programmes, the university also offers UGC-entitled online degree programmes through its Online VGU platform.

As one of the leading online education providers in Rajasthan, Online VGU is committed to delivering affordable and high-quality higher education. The university offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programmes designed to help learners achieve their academic and professional goals while enjoying the flexibility of online learning.

Online VGU enhances the learning experience through advanced digital resources such as an E-Library, AI-based learning tools, and gamified learning modules. The university also maintains strong industry connections, enabling students to participate in workshops, industry interactions, and placement opportunities with leading recruiters from various sectors.

ADMISSION PROCESS

Admission Process

Admissions at Vivekananda Global University Online are conducted entirely online. Eligible candidates can apply directly without appearing for any entrance examination. The admission process is simple, transparent, and designed for the convenience of learners.

1

Complete the online application form using a valid email ID.

2

Pay the registration fee through the online payment portal.

3

Upload and submit all required documents for verification.

4

Wait for admission confirmation through the portal, email, or SMS after successful verification.
