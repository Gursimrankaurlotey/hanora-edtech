University Overview

About Dr. D.Y. Patil Vidyapeeth

Dr DY Patil Vidyapeeth, Pune, is a self-financing non-profit organisation with 13 institutions in the field of Medicine, Dentistry, Nursing, Physiotherapy, Optometry, Biotechnology, Management, Ayurved, Homoeopathy, Designing, Arts, Science & Technology and Health Sciences. Its Centre of Online Learning (COL) is solely dedicated to imparting high-quality education by blending traditional and modern teaching methodologies. Moreover, DY Patil Vidyapeeth Online is accredited with NAAC A++ grade. It has been recognised by both national and international bodies, holding AICTE, UGC, and WES recognition.

The curriculum of the online programmes has been designed by a panel of business professionals, which focuses on case studies and real-world examples. It ensures the academic standards, credibility and pedagogy of its degrees in its bachelor’s, master’s and certificate courses. The list of popular DY Patil online courses includes an online MBA, online MCA, and online BBA. The fee structure of DY Patil Vidyapeeth online courses ranges between INR 28,000 and INR 1,92,400. The university offers one-on-one guidance and mock interviews for career support. Start your journey today with Dr DY Patil Vidyapeeth Online and receive assured placement assistance with 300+ hiring partners.

Scholarships

Scholarships & Financial Benefits

Dr DY Patil Vidyapeeth Online Pune is a private, non-profit, and self-financing organisation. It provides budget-friendly courses that fit most learners' pockets easily. Therefore, the university does not have scholarship provisions for online courses. However, it allows you to avail a discounted amount on payable fees as part of scholarships for online learners. Here is an overview:

Available Scholarship Categories

  • Online BBA Scholarships: A scholarship amount of INR 5,000 would be provided to online BBA learners upon making full fee payment in a single or two transactions within 15 days of admission.
  • Online MBA & MCA Scholarships: Students enrolling in online MBA and MCA can receive a scholarship of INR 10,000 by paying the entire fees in one or two transactions within 15 days of admission.
