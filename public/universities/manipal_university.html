University Overview

About Online Manipal

Manipal University Online, founded in 2021, offers a top-tier online learning experience. With over 50,000 students and 15+ online courses, it is ideal for those who want to upskill. Moreover, Manipal University Online is NAAC-accredited and recognised by UGC, guaranteeing high-quality education. Additionally, Online Manipal is the digital home for all universities, offering UGC-approved online degrees that are equal to on-campus ones. Since its start, Manipal University Online has empowered over 50,000 learners by providing accessible, affordable, and industry-relevant degrees. The university’s commitment to academic excellence is reflected in its 70+ years of success and its reach across 1500+ towns in India.

Moreover, Manipal online courses fees range between INR 80,000 and INR 1,80,000. Manipal University Online would ensure students receive full placement support, from training to job placement drives. Additionally, the university has over 3000 expert faculty members and 500+ recruiters, including Fortune 500 companies, all dedicated to helping you achieve your career goals. Manipal University Online focuses more on making education accessible and flexible for everyone. With courses available in a 100% online format, students can learn at their own pace from anywhere in the world. Additionally, the university offers a range of new-age certifications and industry-relevant degrees, with the latest skills for your CV.

Scholarships

Scholarships & Financial Benefits

Online Manipal scholarships are designed to make quality education affordable and accessible for learners across the globe. The university offers multiple scholarship schemes covering defence personnel, government employees, alumni, differently-abled learners, and meritorious students.

Available Scholarship Categories

  • Merit-Based Scholarship: 10% scholarship for students scoring 80% and above in bachelor's degree.
  • Defence Scholarship: 20% fee concession for serving and retired defence personnel.
  • Divyaang Scholarship: 20% discount for students holding a Government Divyang Certificate.
  • Government Employees Scholarship: 10% fee concession for central and state government employees.
  • Alumni Scholarship: 20% discount for MUJ & SMU alumni and 15% discount for MAHE alumni.

ADMISSION PROCESS

Admission Process

Online Manipal opens admissions for new academic sessions usually in January. Admissions are granted based on eligibility criteria and no entrance examination is required. Follow the steps below to complete the admission process.

1

Visit the official Online Manipal website and register yourself.

2

Fill out the online application form for your preferred course.

3

Upload all required educational and identity documents.

4

Pay the registration fee online and submit the application form.

5

After selection, pay the programme fee to confirm admission.
