University Overview

About Amity University Online

Amity University is the first university in India to gain approval from the UGC to offer online degrees in 2009. Started in 1986 by the Chauhan family, Amity Education Group is a not-for-profit organisation. Today, the university has a presence in 11+ countries with over 1,75,000 students. To cater to the educational needs of a larger segment of individuals, Amity took the initiative to offer education through the online mode.

Amity University Online has a prestigious NAAC A+ accreditation that makes the university renowned across India. Additionally, this is one of the top universities to hold WASC and QAA accreditation, making the Amity online courses highly accepted across the UK and the USA. Recognition from WES also ensures the online courses’ credibility in Canada. Moreover, to ensure that students make the most out of their online learning experience, it has set up a one-of-its-kind platform, AMIGO, that takes care of all the study-related needs of students. From UG and PG to diploma courses, the university caters to a broader range of academic courses.

Amity Online University online courses are available across management, IT, commerce, arts and humanities, etc. disciplines. With strong industry partnerships, the university provides internship opportunities. It helps learners gain hands-on experience, prepare for interviews, and secure promising jobs by connecting with top employers. With its innovative thinking and futuristic approach, Amity University Online is closing the gaps in education by making quality higher education accessible to all.

ADMISSION PROCESS

Admission Process

The admissions process takes place in online mode. Fresh admission starts from the month of January of every year. There are direct admissions, no entrance exam is conducted for the admission process.The addmission procedure for the online course at Amity University Online is as follows:

1

Visit the website to initiate the application process

2

Register using essential details such as Name, Email ID, and Contact Details

3

Receive an email containing the registration number and password

4

Provide information about education and work experience, followed by an undertaking

5

Make the payment with the registration amount of INR 5,500; additional time is provided for pending payments

6

Attach the necessary documents, including 10th and 12th mark sheets, degree certificates, UG mark sheet and degree, Aadhar Card or any government ID, and a photograph
