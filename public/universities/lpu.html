University Overview

About Lovely Professional University ODL (LPU)

Lovely Professional University (LPU) was established in 2005. Today, it is recognised as one of the premier educational institutions in India, securing an A++ rating by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC). To make world-class education accessible to all, the university has set up the Distance Education Centre, which brings great learning opportunities to students. LPU Distance Education provides a wholly digital ecosystem for students to flourish. It offers a variety of undergraduate and postgraduate degrees along with short-duration diploma programmes through open and distance learning modes.

LPU distance education courses are approved by the University Grants Commission & Distance Education Bureau (UGC-DEB). It offers AICTE-recognised distance MBA and MCA degrees, making them highly valid, legitimate, and accepted. Apart from this, LPU is recognised by WES, which makes its degrees recognised in Canada and the USA as well. You can also opt for your desired specialisations in MBA, MCA, and MA-like courses to build expertise in the areas of your interest.

Additionally, quality education provided by the Lovely Professional University Distance Education is highly affordable. The cost of LPU distance courses varies from INR 14,000 to INR 75,000. Furthermore, LPU provides students with the chance to participate in campus events, thus encouraging holistic development. It also provides advanced career support services to help students secure a well-paying job. Several top industry partnerships of LPU Distance Education include Videocon, IntelliPaat, TATA Sky, Airtel, Goldman Sachs, & Myntra, among others.

ADMISSION PROCESS

Admission Process

The admissions to Lovely Professional University ODL courses are conducted twice a year. The admission process is conducted online, virtually. LPU Distance Education typically takes place for the January session & July session. There is no entrance exam conducted, as the candidates are generally shortlisted based on eligibility fulfilment. Check out how to apply for Lovely Professional University distance courses:

1

2

3

4

5

6

Deposit the course fee as instructed online and begin learning.
